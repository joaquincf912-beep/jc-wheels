The risk of buying wheels from random sellers

Cheap wheels and amateur installs cost you more in the long run. Here's why it matters.

Wrong fitment = damage

Wrong bolt pattern, offset, or hub bore can damage your suspension, brakes, and wheel bearings. We check every spec before anything goes on your vehicle.

No warranty, no returns

Facebook Marketplace wheels don't come with a warranty. If they crack, bend, or peel — that's on you. We only sell from brands that stand behind their product.

Bad install = vibrations

Wheels that aren't properly balanced or torqued cause vibrations, uneven tire wear, and can even come loose. Our team uses professional equipment on every job.
